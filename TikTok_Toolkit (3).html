⚡ Flash Sale Steps

  1. Import flash sale items into TikTok Shop from PIM.
  2. Go into Marketing on the left-hand side of Seller Center.
  3. Go into Promotions.
  4. Choose LIVE Promotions, then LIVE flash sale, then Create.
  5. Make the Promotion Name “MM/DD/2026 Flash Sale 1/18” (e.g. 07/20/2026 Flash Sale 1/18).
  6. Choose Schedule start date (pick start date and time).
  7. Set duration to 20 minutes (LIVE flash sale duration must be between 1 and 20 minutes).
  8. Keep Product-level selected, press Select products.
  9. Select one item using TikTok’s SKU, then press Done.
  10. Set the flash price for the item you just selected.
  11. After all flash items are listed, click Agree and publish.
  12. Click Manage promotions.
  13. Click Duplicate on the right side of the flash sale you just created.
  14. Change the promotion name to match the correct date and number.
  15. Change the date and time. Each flash sale starts one second after the previous one ends. Then Agree and publish.
  16. Repeat until all 18 are done.

🔨 Auction List Tasks (JTV TikTok Shop)

  1. Add a new Cost column to the Brian and Lois Excel sheets. Cost is found in MicroStrategy.
  2. Create two new Excel documents (one for Brian, one for Lois) in the model format: SKU, Image, Description, P1 Price, Auction Price.
  3. Keep 2 copies of each document: one with Cost, and one in the model format.
  4. Add all SKUs using the TikTok JTV product eligibility in PIM and export like normal. Auction products do not need to be unchecked — keep them checked in PIM.
  5. Add all SKUs into Auction in JTV TikTok Seller Center.
  6. In the original list, SKU is Column B (marked Product ID) and the bid price is Column L (“Bid Price”).

Starting bid rule (applies to ALL three lists)

Auction Starting Bid Price = Cost + $10. Example: cost $14 → starting bid $24.

🚫 Restricted Categories

Restricted products are most likely in one of these two categories:

  1. Gold
  2. Ruby, Emerald & Sapphire

Gold products

Must be moved to Platinum, Carat Gold. Search “carat” in the Category box.
Example: Gold – Gold Necklaces & Pendants → Platinum & Carat Gold Necklaces & Pendants.

Ruby, Emerald & Sapphire products

Must be changed to Artificial Gemstones. Search “artificial” in the Category box, then pick the matching type: necklace → Necklaces, bracelet → Bracelets.

Pick the category under Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives – Artificial Gemstones, not “Home Supplies – Home Decor – Decorative Flowers… Artificial”.
